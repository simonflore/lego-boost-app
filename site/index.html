Boost Control

Drive your LEGO® BOOST robot from an iPhone or iPad, with a real game controller.

Beta on TestFlight: coming soon Free · iPhone and iPad · iOS 16.4 or later

What it does

Game controllers

Drive with a Stadia, PlayStation, Xbox or MFi controller: sticks to steer, triggers for throttle, buttons for the arm.

Every BOOST build

Presets for all five models in set 17101, so the motors and buttons match what you built.

Obstacle mode

Uses the colour & distance sensor to drive on its own and back off before it bumps into things.

Hub at a glance

Battery level, distance, colour and tilt, plus hub light colours and direct control of every motor.

Works with

  • Vernie
  • M.T.R.4
  • Frankie the Cat
  • Guitar 4000
  • AutoBuilder
  • Your own build

Controller layout

InputAction
Left stickDrive and steer
R2 / L2Throttle forward / reverse
D-padFull-power drive and turn
Right stickMotor D: arm, shooter or head
A / ✕Short burst on motor D
B / ○Stop everything
Y / △Change the hub light
L1 / R1Speed limit down / up

Join the beta

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the TestFlight link above on your iPhone or iPad and tap Accept.
  3. Pair your controller in Settings → Bluetooth, turn on the hub and press Connect.
