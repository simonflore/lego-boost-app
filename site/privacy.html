Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version

Boost Control collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app doesn't send any data to its developer or to anyone else.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find and talk to your BOOST Move Hub. Motor commands and sensor readings (battery, distance, colour, tilt) travel only between your device and the hub. They aren't stored or uploaded.

Game controllers

Controller input is read on your device through Apple's GameController framework and turned straight into motor commands. It isn't recorded.

Settings

Your chosen robot model and fine-tuning are saved on your device so they're still there next time. Deleting the app deletes them.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you send with the developer, as described in Apple's privacy policy. The app itself adds nothing to that.

Children

The app is meant for families building with BOOST. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
