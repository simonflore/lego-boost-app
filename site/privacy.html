Privacy

Collects nothing

Last updated 15 November 2026.

No data, no accounts

No sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app and the web version send nothing to us or anyone else.

Bluetooth

Used only to talk to your BOOST Move Hub. Motor commands and sensor readings (battery, distance, colour, tilt) stay between your device and the hub.

Controllers

Button presses are read on your device and turned straight into motor commands. Nothing is recorded.

Settings

Your robot model and fine-tuning are saved on your device. Delete the app (or clear the site’s data) and they’re gone.

TestFlight

If you test the iPhone and iPad beta, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you send, under Apple’s privacy policy. The app adds nothing to that.

Kids

Made for families who build with BOOST. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes and questions

Changes show up here with a new date. Questions go to GitHub issues.
